📈 Rating Insights & Analytical Takeaways

Key findings uncovered from rigorous data analysis.

🎭

1. Niche vs. Mainstream Genres

Niche categories achieve higher averages because they attract dedicated audiences.

⏳

2. Survivorship Bias in Older Films

Only the most beloved masterpieces continue to be watched and rated today.

📊

3. The Long-Tail Power Law

Top movies capture over 50% of all audience ratings.

⚠️

4. Voluntary Response Bias Disclaimer

Ratings reflect enthusiastic voluntary reviewers rather than a randomized survey.

ℹ️ About CineScope 2.0

Academic Project: Movie Ratings and Genre Trends (Project ID: DA-006)

📂 Dataset Documentation

  • Source: GroupLens Research (University of Minnesota) & Bollywood Dataset
  • Ratings: 100,836+ individual user submissions
  • Movies: 9,772+ total films (Hollywood + Hindi Classics)
  • Time Span: 1902 to 2024
  • Scale: 0.5 to 5.0 stars with 0.5 step increments
